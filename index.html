Explain the Model

How three regression models turn a person's measurements into one prediction, using real NHANES survey data.

1. Push & Pull (linear regression) 2. Wisdom of the Forest (random forest) 3. One Correction at a Time (gradient boosting)
Data: CDC/NCHS National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES). All model numbers come from the precomputed course bundle (models fitted on the training rows only). The example people are validation participants the models never trained on. Everything here describes associations in the data, not causes.
